# Walk-forward validation

Open **Historical replay → Open bar backtest** and select the demo or import a session calendar and OHLCV CSV. The walk-forward panel uses the account and execution policy from the latest successful bar backtest. Apply changes to those settings with **Run bar backtest**, then set a walk-forward observation cutoff, training length, test length, and comma-separated candidate pairs such as 1:2, 2:3. Run the validation and select a completed fold to inspect its training ranks and test equity.

Each fold uses a fixed-length rolling training window followed by a disjoint test window. The step between folds equals the test length. Lengths count observed bar closes from the session calendar, so a missing interval contributes no bar. A fold appears only after the final test close is observable. Each candidate is run on that fold's training closes under the same starting cash, risk limits, fees and execution policy. Ending equity determines rank using exact fixed-point values. A tie selects the lower fast window, then the lower slow window.

The winning windows stay fixed for the full test. Prior training closes warm the moving averages and seed the previous comparison. The account starts each test with its configured cash and no orders or positions. A signal on the first test close can place an order eligible for liquidity at a later close. Test folds report separate results and do not compound account balances. The underlying API returns the complete equity marks, signals and paper receipts for each test. The panel shows training equity, drawdown and accepted fills for every candidate, plus test ending equity, drawdown and a chart.

The demo uses six training and two test bars. At 14:08 UTC on 2025-04-03, no fold is complete. At 14:09, the first fold reveals training equity of 1003.4 for 1:2 and 997.8 for 2:3. Its training stops at 14:07 and its test spans 14:08 to 14:09. At 14:11, a second test covering 14:10 to 14:11 is visible. The first fold's choice and results remain identical. The native test changes the first test prices and demonstrates unchanged training scores and a changed test trace.

The source holds up to 5,000 bar rows. Supply 1–64 distinct pairs with a positive fast window below the slow window, and a slow window at most 1,000 and below the training length. Training and testing together may use up to two million bar evaluations per run. This milestone validates a single-instrument SMA search.
